# GLM-5.3-Flash decode profile, 2026-10-02

Tree: `1aaef41` (TensorFold 0.6.1 + MiaAI-Lab 53-patch stack). Two GB10 Sparks, TP=2, `--parallel 4`,
`TF_GLM_MULTI_LONE=0`, `CONTEXT=524288`, embedders loaded (regime "loaded", about 3.5% below quiet).
Capture: `nsys launch -t cuda,nvtx,osrt --cuda-graph-trace=node` on rank 0, `nsys start/stop` around
`lean_bench.py` (4 single-stream requests, 400 tokens each, 4 bursts of about 8.1 s). Analysis: `tools/dev/analyze_nsys.py`.
Single request throughput under the profiler: 48.3 prose, 50.8 code (not representative, tracing overhead).

## Where the wall time goes (rank 0, 33.5 s span, 4 requests)

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Union-of-streams GPU busy | 93.3% of the span; inside the 4 decode bursts, idle is 5.2% (1.70 s) |
| Idle gaps under 5 us | 434k gaps, 310 ms total (graph-internal, nothing to gain) |
| Idle gaps 100 us - 10 ms | 2,576 gaps, 1.37 s total, about 4.2% of burst time (host turnaround around draft / sample / commit) |
| Rounds | about 485 (dec_kernel calls / 84), so about 3.3 tokens per round and about 67 ms per round |

Reading: per-layer launch fusion cannot win much, because there are no mid-size gaps inside a round. The ceiling for
removing every host turnaround is about +4%. The rest is GPU-bound weight streaming.

`dec_kernel` is about 16.2 s of the 33.5 s wall on rank 0, about 33 ms of every 67 ms round.

## Consequences for the loop

1. Host-side work is capped at about +4%; the draft/sample/commit turnaround is the only target there.
2. The leverage is bytes: `dec_kernel` throughput, the number of distinct experts read per accepted token, and what
   `l2pf_kernel` (17% of kernel time, overlapped) costs the streaming kernels.
3. Fusing the hyper-connection chain (about 2.5% of kernel time in total) cannot beat the noise floor on its own.

Open: `group_kernel` is not yet identified (81k calls, 12.8%); achieved DRAM bandwidth of `dec_kernel` is not yet measured.
